ABOUT SWP

Built on quality. Driven by scale.

Established in 2000 in Tirunelveli, Tamil Nadu, Sabarimala Wire & Wire Products has grown into a fencing materials manufacturer and supplier serving residential, commercial, agricultural and industrial requirements.

Our focus is simple — reliable materials, dependable execution and fencing solutions designed to perform for years.

Mission : Our mission is to empower our customers with reliable and innovative fencing solutions that enhance safety, define boundaries, and add value to their properties.
